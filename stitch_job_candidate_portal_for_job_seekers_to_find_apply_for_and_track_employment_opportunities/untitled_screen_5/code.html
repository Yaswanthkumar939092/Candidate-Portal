Job Description

Senior Software Engineer

Acme Corp · Remote · Full-time

Engineering Full-time

About the job

We are seeking a highly skilled Senior Software Engineer to join our dynamic team. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in software development, a passion for innovation, and the ability to lead and mentor junior engineers. This role offers the opportunity to work on cutting-edge projects and contribute to the growth of a fast-paced technology company.

Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and maintain high-quality software solutions.

  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define, design, and ship new features.

  • Lead and mentor junior engineers, providing technical guidance and support.

  • Participate in code reviews and contribute to improving team processes.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field.

  • 5+ years of experience in software development.

  • Proficiency in Java, Python, or C++.

  • Strong problem-solving and communication skills.

About the company

Acme Corp is a leading technology company specializing in innovative software solutions for various industries. We are committed to fostering a collaborative and inclusive work environment where employees can thrive and make a meaningful impact.
